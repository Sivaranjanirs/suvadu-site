Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

We measure whether Suvadu works. We do not collect the contents of your memories, voice recordings, or search questions.

Suvadu is a voice-first memory app. You tap the microphone, say something you want to remember, and Suvadu turns it into searchable text so you can find it later. Privacy is a core part of how Suvadu is built.

Your memories

  • Your memories are stored privately on your device using Apple’s on-device storage.
  • Speech is transcribed on your device when your device and language support it. The temporary audio recording is deleted after a memory is saved — Suvadu keeps the text, not a voice archive.
  • If you enable iCloud sync, your memories are stored in your own private iCloud account so they stay in sync across your Apple devices, using Apple’s iCloud/CloudKit. The developer cannot see the contents of your private iCloud data.
  • Your memories are never used for advertising.

Analytics

To understand whether Suvadu is reliable and useful, the app sends anonymous, aggregate usage signals to our analytics provider, TelemetryDeck. These signals describe how the app is used, never what you record or search for.

Examples of what is sent: a recording started/completed/failed (with a coarse duration range and a general failure category); whether it was your first memory and a rough count range; a search opened/submitted/completed and whether it returned results; app version, OS version, and device model supplied by the analytics SDK.

Never sent to analytics:

  • your memory text or any part of a transcript
  • your voice or audio
  • your search questions
  • retrieved memories or AI-generated answers
  • names, people, places, addresses, phone numbers, or dates from your memories
  • topics, categories, or any content-derived classification
  • memory identifiers, iCloud identifiers, your name, email, or any account identity
  • your location

Analytics data is anonymous and is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites. TelemetryDeck’s privacy information: telemetrydeck.com/privacy.

On-device intelligence

When your device supports Apple’s on-device model, Suvadu may generate a short answer to your question. This happens on your device using only your retrieved memories. Your memories are not sent to any cloud AI service.

Deleting your data

You can delete all of your memories at any time from Account → Delete All Memories. If iCloud sync is on, deletion also removes them from your other signed-in devices. Anonymous analytics signals contain no memory content and no identity.

Children

Suvadu is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
